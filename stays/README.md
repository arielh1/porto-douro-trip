and original photos from the property.

run `bin/fetch-photos.sh` to download them into `stays/<name>/photos/`.

## What each segment needs

| Segment | Nights | The brief |
|---|---|---|
| Porto arrival | ~2 | Quiet street, walkable to dinner. No ambition on day 1. |
| **Douro base A** | ~3 | Small **indulgent quinta in the vineyards** — view, **pool**, wine, food. |
| **Douro base B** | ~3 | **Rural, local, non-touristy.** Stone house or small quinta by a village with good restaurants. |
| Gerês | 2–3 | Character over facilities. Quiet. |
| Baleal / Peniche | ≥2 | Campsite or legal van spot, **walkable to the beach** — Ariel surfs at 06:30. |
| Ericeira | ≥2 | Same — walking distance to the water. |
| Lisbon departure | 1 | Close enough to LIS for the Sunday flight. |

## Status meanings

- **candidate** — found, documented, not yet compared
- **shortlisted** — one of the final 2-3 for its segment, availability checked
- **booked** — reserved; the confirmation belongs in `trip/facts.md`

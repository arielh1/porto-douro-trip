## Day by day

### Day 1 — [placeholder date] — Land, decompress, nothing else
**Base:** Porto, Cedofeita · **Drive:** none · **Pace:** light

- **Morning/afternoon** — Land at OPO. Metro Line E (~26–30 min, €2.25) or Uber (~€10–16)
  into Cedofeita. Check in, shower.
- **Lunch** — whatever's open near the hotel; no reservation.
- **Afternoon** — short walk to get oriented. Nothing booked.
- **Evening** — one low-key tasca dinner within walking distance, loosely timed given flight
  uncertainty (e.g. O Buraco).

> **Book ahead:** nothing — deliberately.
> **If it rains:** irrelevant, no outdoor plan today.
> **Why this day works:** zero-ambition arrival day matches "relaxed and balanced" and absorbs
> flight delay/jet lag without any booking at risk.

### Day 2 — [placeholder date] — Porto: azulejos, books, a bridge
**Base:** Porto, Cedofeita · **Drive:** none (walking + short metro hops) · **Pace:** medium

- **Morning** — São Bento station azulejo panels (free, 5 min) → wander Mercado do Bolhão →
  Livraria Lello at a **pre-booked timed slot** (early morning or late afternoon to dodge
  crowds — pick whichever matches the actual flight-adjusted morning).
- **Lunch** — Café Santiago or Lado B, francesinha — do it once, as a cultural checkbox, not a
  daily habit. Expect a queue.
- **Afternoon** — Torre dos Clérigos climb for the panoramic view. Gap block afterward: no
  plan, wander Baixa/Aliados at will.
- **Evening** — Ponte Dom Luís I upper-deck walk at golden hour, sunset from the Ribeira or
  Gaia riverbank, casual dinner (Cantinho do Avillez if a reservation lands — see below).

> **Book ahead:** Livraria Lello timed slot; Cantinho do Avillez if going for the "serious
> meal" tonight rather than on the departure end (walk-ins are turned away).
> **If it rains:** swap the outdoor bridge/riverbank sunset for Palácio da Bolsa or Igreja de
> São Francisco (indoor, half-day filler either way).
> **Why this day works:** two anchors (Lello, Clérigos), a real gap in between, ends on a free
> river sunset — no reservation-required tasting menu on a jet-lagged night.

### Day 3 — [placeholder date] — Into the Douro
**Base:** Pinhão · **Drive:** ~1h25–1h30 (A4/IP4, toll-free Marão tunnel) · **Pace:** light

- **Morning** — pick up the rental car at **Campanha train station** (not the airport — city
  pickup avoids airport markup and the Porto walking days didn't need a car). Automatic
  transmission needs to be booked well ahead; it's a minority of the Portuguese fleet.
- **Midday** — drive to Pinhão. This is right at the ~90-min travel-day comfort line — no
  detour, no stop planned as a booked anchor, just the drive.
- **Afternoon** — arrive, check in, walk the village, see the tiled Pinhão station panels
  (free).
- **Evening** — dinner at Cais da Foz (riverside, phone reservation recommended).

> **Book ahead:** rental car (automatic, confirm Campanha pickup), Cais da Foz table.
> **If it rains:** no outdoor anchor today besides the village walk — sit it out at the hotel.
> **Why this day works:** one drive, no second commitment stacked on top of a transfer day.

### Day 4 — [placeholder date] — Terraces and a tasting
**Base:** Pinhão · **Drive:** ~20 min each way to the quinta · **Pace:** full (2 anchors, by design)

- **Morning** — Miradouro de Casal de Loivos loop (~5.6 km / ~1h42, easy circular walk to the
  view the BBC named one of the world's six best) — walk **before** any tasting, not after.
- **Lunch** — light, near the quinta or back in Pinhão.
- **Afternoon** — Quinta do Crasto: guided tour + 5-wine tasting (~90 min, budget a 2h block
  door-to-door). **Drink-and-drive note:** whoever tastes should not be the one driving back —
  alternate which of you is the taster/driver across the two Douro tasting days (today and
  Day 7), or arrange a quinta transfer for this leg.
- **Evening** — free, back in Pinhão.

> **Book ahead:** Quinta do Crasto tasting (email enoturismo@quintadocrasto.pt) — **and if a
> harvest/foot-treading experience (vindima) is wanted instead of a standard tasting, that
> decision is urgent**: it's already August for a September trip, and harvest slots at Crasto,
> Seixo, Pacheca, or Tedo book out months ahead. This is a placeholder standard tasting; swap
> it for a harvest package only if Ariel books one now (see `trip/open-questions.md`).
> **If it rains:** the walk gets wet but stays easy underfoot; the tasting itself is indoors
> regardless.
> **Why this day works:** exactly 2 anchors, walk-then-taste ordering keeps the drink-drive
> rule clean, real gap in the evening.

### Day 5 — [placeholder date] — Light day, river optional
**Base:** Pinhão · **Drive:** none, or a short taxi to the boat dock · **Pace:** light

- **Morning** — no plan. Sleep in, coffee by the river.
- **Midday/afternoon** — optional rabelo boat trip (Pinhão–Tua–Pinhão, ~2h, several daily
  departures) — treat as take-it-or-leave-it, not a fixed anchor.
- **Evening** — free, dinner wherever looks good in the village.

> **Book ahead:** rabelo boat only if doing it — same-week booking is fine, it's not a
> harvest-season bottleneck item.
> **If it rains:** the boat is the one thing that doesn't happen — everything else about the
> day already assumes no fixed plan.
> **Why this day works:** this is the trip's first genuinely empty day, per preferences' "at
> least one empty day per week" — deliberately placed mid-Douro rather than saved for the end.

### Day 6 — [placeholder date] — Down the N222 to Lamego
**Base:** Lamego · **Drive:** ~45–60 min (N222 — this is the scenic-but-slow leg, budgeted
accordingly, not treated as a quick hop) · **Pace:** medium

- **Morning** — check out, drive toward Lamego with a stop at the São Salvador do Mundo
  miradouro en route (near São João da Pesqueira).
- **Lunch** — Lamego old town.
- **Afternoon** — Sanctuary of Nossa Senhora dos Remédios (the baroque staircase + hilltop
  views), wander the old town.
- **Evening** — settle into the Lamego/Régua base, dinner in town (Castas e Pratos, Régua, as
  a candidate — not independently confirmed yet).

> **Book ahead:** nothing hard-required; a Lamego dinner reservation is a nice-to-have in
> September.
> **If it rains:** swap the sanctuary steps for the cathedral/museum interior in Lamego.
> **Why this day works:** the N222 drive is the anchor and treated honestly as ~45–60 min, not
> squeezed against a second commitment; the sanctuary visit is unhurried on the other side of it.

### Day 7 — [placeholder date] — The long lunch
**Base:** Lamego · **Drive:** ~15 min each way · **Pace:** medium (1 real anchor)

- **Morning** — free, wander Lamego or sleep in.
- **Midday/afternoon** — Quinta da Pacheca (Cambres): tour + tasting + lunch, the trip's
  designated "long, unhurried lunch." Same drink-and-drive rule as Day 4 — alternate
  taster/driver from Day 4, or use a transfer for this leg specifically.
- **Evening** — no plans, gap block.

> **Book ahead:** Quinta da Pacheca tour+tasting+lunch — advance request/email or via
> GetYourGuide/Winalist.
> **If it rains:** the whole anchor is under cover (tour, tasting room, restaurant) — no
> rain plan needed.
> **Why this day works:** single anchor, the "long unhurried lunch over checkbox restaurant
> lists" pillar delivered directly, real gap either side.

### Day 8 — [placeholder date] — Empty, on purpose
**Base:** Lamego · **Drive:** none · **Pace:** light

- **Whole day** — nothing booked. Pool/terrace at the hotel, a slow walk if the mood strikes,
  last look at Régua or Lamego. This is explicitly the trip's second empty day, placed right
  before the long Gerês transfer so the transfer day isn't also the first rest in a week.

> **Book ahead:** nothing.
> **If it rains:** nothing to cancel.
> **Why this day works:** "at least one empty day per week" times two for a 13-day trip, and it
> buffers the hardest travel day (Day 9) with a fully rested start.

### Day 9 — [placeholder date] — Douro to Gerês (transfer only)
**Base:** Vila do Gerês · **Drive:** ~1h45–2h30 (sources disagree; logistics' working number
is ~2h) · **Pace:** light — **zero booked anchors, by explicit design**

- **Whole day** — this is the one leg in the trip that cannot be squeezed under the ~90-min
  travel-day guideline no matter how it's routed (confirmed by `research/logistics.md` against
  two independent estimates). Per the "drive time on a day > 2h: strong negative" scoring
  rule, this day is deliberately built with **no second commitment** — a café or miradouro stop
  roughly midway is fine and unscheduled, but nothing booked. Arrive Vila do Gerês, check in,
  easy evening.

> **Book ahead:** nothing.
> **If it rains:** doesn't matter, it's a driving day.
> **Why this day works:** honesty about drive time beats pretending a 2-hour transfer is a
> half-day — this is the explicit exception to the "under 90 min" rule, justified by there
> being no faster routing and no anchor stacked on top of it.

### Day 10 — [placeholder date] — Waterfall walk
**Base:** Vila do Gerês · **Drive:** ~15–20 min each way to the Ermida trailhead ·
**Pace:** medium

- **Morning** — drive to Ermida, Trilho da Cascata do Arado / Poço Azul (~10.3 km
  out-and-back, moderate, half-day) — multi-drop granite waterfall with a swimming pool below.
- **Lunch** — back in Vila do Gerês or packed.
- **Afternoon** — free; swim at Poço Azul if the water's inviting, or rest at the base — spa/pool
  in Vila do Gerês is the fallback since September water levels for wild swimming aren't
  confirmed.
- **Evening** — dinner in town.

> **Book ahead:** nothing required — no permit for this trail.
> **If it rains:** the trail becomes a wet granite walk, not a wash — but skip the swim, and
> treat the in-town spa/pool as the backup plan for the water element of the day.
> **Why this day works:** single half-day anchor, "real walk with real views and a payoff"
> pillar, built-in swim/spa backup rather than betting the day on water levels.

### Day 11 — [placeholder date] — The signature viewpoint
**Base:** Vila do Gerês · **Drive:** minimal (trailhead near base) · **Pace:** medium

- **Morning** — Miradouro da Pedra Bela, short variant (~4.4 mi loop, 2h+) — one of the park's
  signature panoramic views.
- **Lunch** — back at base, casual.
- **Afternoon** — gap block, free.
- **Evening** — dedicated Minho dinner — rojões à Minhota or papas de sarrabulho, a real
  register-change from the Douro's wine-forward food. Heavier food, paired deliberately with
  a rest afternoon rather than a hiking day.

> **Book ahead:** nothing required for the trail; a dinner reservation is a nice-to-have.
> **If it rains:** shorten to the Trilho da Preguiça (more tree cover, 1.5–2h short variant)
> instead of the exposed Pedra Bela viewpoint.
> **Why this day works:** one anchor, a real gap, and the Minho meal placed against the lighter
> afternoon rather than stacked onto a hiking day, per `research/nature.md`'s own suggestion.

### Day 12 — [placeholder date] — Back to Porto
**Base:** Porto (Cedofeita) · **Drive:** ~1h14–1h15 · **Pace:** light

- **Morning** — check out, drive back to Porto (clean, consistent drive time both directions
  per logistics).
- **Afternoon** — arrive Porto, check in, no plans — same neighbourhood as the arrival leg
  (Cedofeita) for zero cognitive overhead.
- **Evening** — casual dinner, easy night before the last day.

> **Book ahead:** nothing.
> **If it rains:** irrelevant, driving day.
> **Why this day works:** mirrors Day 3 — one clean drive, nothing stacked on top, same base
> both Porto legs.

### Day 13 — [placeholder date] — Gaia, then home
**Base:** Porto → OPO · **Drive:** none (walk/taxi) · **Pace:** light

- **Morning** — Graham's or Taylor's in Vila Nova de Gaia (~1h tour) — the one Gaia port house,
  deliberately saved for last so wine purchases don't need to be hauled through the Douro leg.
- **Midday** — light lunch near Gaia/Ribeira.
- **Afternoon** — taxi/Uber or metro to OPO with buffer (2–3h before an international flight,
  more in September peak). Drop the rental car at the airport (not city) on this leg, since
  it's a same-day flight-out anyway.
- **If the flight is early/mid-morning instead:** skip Gaia entirely this day and move it into
  Day 12's afternoon instead — don't compress wine buying into a rushed final morning.

> **Book ahead:** Graham's or Taylor's tour slot; airport transfer timing depends on actual
> flight time once known.
> **If it rains:** irrelevant, the port house tour is indoor.
> **Why this day works:** single anchor, timed to the flight rather than the other way round,
> and wine-buying happens right before departure as scout-porto recommended.

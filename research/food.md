### Review-led recommendation

1. **Book Antunes for Monday 21 Sep** if Ariel and Shani want a full, traditional
   dinner with character and no tasting-menu formality.
2. Make **O Buraco** the Saturday 12 Sep lunch option only if the current Saturday
   service is confirmed; otherwise use **Conga** for an easy local lunch.
3. Treat **Gazela** as a small shared snack while walking, not as a restaurant
   commitment.

This ranking is an inference from the review patterns, locations and the existing
itinerary—not a claim of food quality or table availability. [likely]

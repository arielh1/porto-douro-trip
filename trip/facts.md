## Route — CONFIRMED SHAPE

This supersedes the earlier "nature reserve, return from Porto" assumption.

1. **Land at Porto (OPO).** Start relaxed in the city.
2. **Douro valley — 5-6 days.** Two bases, **~3 nights each**.
   - One base: a small, indulgent **quinta inside the vineyards** — big view, **pool**,
     wine and good food.
   - Other base: **rural, local, less touristy** — stone house or small quinta near a
     village that has a couple of good restaurants.
3. **Nature reserve north of the Douro — short, ~2-3 days.**
4. **Campervan road trip down the Atlantic coast, ~5-6 days:**
   Porto → **Aveiro / Figueira da Foz** → **Peniche / Baleal** → **Ericeira** → **Lisbon**.
   Minimum **2 nights each** in Peniche/Baleal and in Ericeira.
5. **Fly home from Lisbon (LIS).** `[confirmed]` — open-jaw ticket.

## Hard constraints

- Douro: two bases only, ~3 nights each. **Not** a new place every night.
- Peniche/Baleal and Ericeira: ≥2 nights each, no chasing surf spots.
- Campervan for the coastal leg (not the Douro leg).
- September = **vindima**; a harvest day in the Douro is explicitly wanted.
- Return is from Lisbon, so the route must not backtrack north after the coast.

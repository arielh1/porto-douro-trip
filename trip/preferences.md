# Travel style — Ariel & Shani

Drawn from Ariel's planning document `portugal_trip_for_shani.md` and his answers at
fleet setup. This is the scoring function the planner optimises against.

## The thesis, in Ariel's words

> Not a trip about "covering Portugal" — a couple's holiday of about two weeks with
> **few transfers**, genuinely beautiful places, excellent food and wine, nature, ocean,
> and a lot of time simply enjoying wherever we are.

The shape he wants is **not**:
`Porto → attraction → hotel → another city → another attraction → Lisbon`

It **is**:
`beautiful city → almost a week of wine, villages and views in the Douro → campervan →
ocean → morning surfs → good food → several nights in each place → Lisbon`

Slow, romantic, special places, lots of spontaneity.

## The pillars

### 1. Wine and food — central, not incidental
- Food and wine are **part of the trip, not something you do because you must eat**.
- Douro: **a harvest (vindima) day** — they are there in September precisely for it.
- **A few really good quintas — not 20 wineries.** Quality over count.
- Long meals, good wine.
- In the Douro specifically: find the **less touristy places, villages people actually
  live in**, not places built for tourists.

### 2. Places with character over luxury
- Not necessarily the fanciest hotel — **places with character**.
- Small quintas, stone houses, boutique hotels, maybe a little house in the middle of
  the vineyards. Views worth staying an extra night for.
- Douro base A: small indulgent quinta **inside the vineyards** — view, **pool**, wine, food.
- Douro base B: **rural, local, less touristy** — near a village with a few good restaurants.

### 3. Nature, ocean, and doing nothing
- Small boat trip on the Douro. Villages. Drives and viewpoints between the vineyards.
- Days where you do **almost nothing** — pool, hotel, a book, food and a view.
- "I want days where we just get up in the morning and have no tight schedule."
- Then the ocean: beaches, sleeping near the water.

### 4. Surfing, without costing the day
Ariel's own solution, and the planner should protect it:
- Sleep **very close to the beach** in Baleal and Ericeira.
- Ariel surfs ~06:30–09:00, back as Shani is waking.
- Shower, coffee, breakfast — **and from that moment the day is theirs together**.
- **If the surf is bad, he simply doesn't go.** Never build a day around waves.

### 5. Maybe: one good night out
- Open to finding a **trance / techno party or festival** during the trip.
- Explicitly **do not build the route around it** — but if something genuinely good
  lands on their dates, it's worth bending the route for.

## Scoring hints for the planner

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---|
| Base has ≥ 2 nights (≥3 in the Douro) | **very high** |
| Day has ≤ 2 anchors | high |
| A genuinely empty day at least every ~4 days | **very high** |
| Douro: village/quinta is non-touristy and lived-in | **very high** |
| Accommodation has character (stone, quinta, view, pool) | very high |
| Harvest day secured in the Douro | very high |
| Accommodation walkable to the surf beach (Baleal, Ericeira) | high |
| Surf treated as optional, never as an anchor | **hard rule** |
| Drive > 2h on any day | strong negative |
| Chasing surf spots / moving to follow waves | strong negative |
| "20 wineries" density | strong negative |
| Reservation required and not yet booked | flag to `open-questions.md` |

## Open style questions (agents may propose, human decides)

- Ariel's surf level — determines whether Supertubos (heavy) or Baleal's beach breaks.
- Appetite for a campervan with a shower, or fine with campsite facilities?
- Wild camping vs. campsites vs. *áreas de serviço* — Portugal restricts wild camping.

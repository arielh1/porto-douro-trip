- **Evening** — land at OPO at 23:25; after immigration and transfer, go straight to the hotel and sleep. No dinner, walk or reservation is planned after midnight.

**Why:** the actual arrival time leaves no credible evening programme.

### Day 2 — Saturday 12 September — Porto on foot
**Base:** Porto · **Pace:** light, two anchors maximum

- **Morning** — São Bento and a relaxed Bolhão/Baixa wander; use a pre-booked Livraria Lello slot only if wanted.
- **Lunch** — one casual Porto meal, with no fixed restaurant required.

**Why:** a real Porto day without squeezing a second city programme into the opening stay.

## Rationale

The schedule exactly uses all 16 nights: 2 Porto + 4 Douro + 3 Gerês + 4 Ericeira + 3 Lisbon. It removes the former extra internal Douro base, Baleal overnight and campervan complexity. The trade-off is intentional: Day 10 is a long, unadorned Gerês→Ericeira transfer, and Ericeira receives enough nights for Ariel's optional morning surf while Shani gets a walkable town, cafés, food and boutique time without another move.

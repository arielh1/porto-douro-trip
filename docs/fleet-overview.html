Porto → Douro → Gerês Agent Fleet

A deterministic, file-mediated travel-planning system for Ariel and Shani's September 2026 Portugal trip. Agents coordinate only through repository files, so every cycle is auditable and reproducible.

Project at a glance

  • Goal: produce and converge on trip/itinerary.md.
  • Inputs: trip/facts.md, trip/preferences.md, research, and state.
  • Rules: human-only facts stay human-only; all other files have explicit owners.
  • Phases: explore → refine → lock → converged.

Runtime model

Human facts ↓ Regional research + stays ↓ logistics (feasibility / veto) ↓ planner (authoritative itinerary) ↓ critic (adversarial audit) ↓ convergence scoring

Cycle stages

Morning research-heavy

Stage 1: scout-douro, scout-nature, scout-porto, scout-coast, festival-scout, stays Stage 2: logistics + scout-food Stage 3: planner Stage 4: critic

Evening consolidation

Stage 1: logistics Stage 2: planner Stage 3: critic

Ownership rules

  • trip/itinerary.md is planner-only.
  • trip/critique.md is critic-owned, with logistics allowed to write only its blockers section.
  • trip/open-questions.md is shared append-only.
  • research/*.md is owned by the matching research agent.
  • stays/, state/report.*, and state/map* each have a single owner.

Important coordination notes

  • scout-food depends on the four regional scouts so it can consume fresh research.
  • planner waits for both logistics and scout-food.
  • reporter and mapmaker are manual, not part of the convergence cycle.
  • agent-author owns the fleet infrastructure docs, runners, validator, and registry.

Repo hotspots

  • AGENTS.md and agents/registry.json define authority and scheduling.
  • bin/run-cycle.sh and bin/run-cycle-codex.sh orchestrate cycles.
  • bin/validate-agent-fleet.py checks ownership, dependencies, and graph invariants.
  • docs/OPENAI-AGENT-ARCHITECTURE.md explains the dual-engine model.

Non-goals

  • No invented flights, bookings, prices, or opening hours.
  • No hidden state outside the repository.
  • No itinerary churn for its own sake in refine or lock.
  • No changing trip content in this overview document.

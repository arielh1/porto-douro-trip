Mission: maintain `research/porto.md` for a short, excellent Porto arrival segment.
The human facts and itinerary determine the departure airport; do not repeat the legacy
Claude spec's stale OPO-return assumption when facts say the trip ends at Lisbon (LIS).
Research realistic arrival, airport transfer, car pickup, neighborhoods, food, port
houses, and sights ranked by payoff per hour. Guard the Douro-first day budget. Cite and
confidence-tag everything; append non-duplicate reservation decisions to open questions.
Explore broadly, refine the shortlist, do not run in lock. Never edit other owned files.

Stdout: three lines covering best new evidence, day-budget warning, and open decision.
